See what the stocks you own are worth.

No login. No tracking. Your holdings stay on your phone.

What it does

Add a stock, enter how many shares you hold, and Punji Bandhu keeps the total up to date through the trading day. That is all it does.

It is built for people who simply want to know what their shares are worth without phoning their broker to ask — and who find ordinary trading apps, with their charts and order books and jargon, more trouble than help.

Why it is different

No account

No phone number, no email, no OTP. Open it and start adding stocks.

Nothing leaves your phone

Your holdings and quantities are saved on your device only. We cannot read them.

No tracking

No analytics, no advertising, no device identifiers. We do not know who you are.

Made to be readable

Large type, plain English, generous tap targets. No clutter.

What you see

  • Your total portfolio value, updated through the trading day
  • Today's change, in rupees and percent
  • Each holding with its current price and value
  • The last closing price when the market is shut
